Here is my BSC Research Project

Pitch Extraction Using DCT Based Power Spectrum Method in Noisy Speech

Supervised By: Dr. Md.Saifur Rahman, Associate Professor, Comilla University

A research conduct to develop a efficient method of extracting Fundamental Frequency of human voice using Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) which enhanced my ability to apply scientific & mathematical theory to quantitative data.Computer based research project, includes MATLAB for coding, simulation & visual representation of data as well as excel for conducting performance & accuracy measurement. Each investigation involves a written report in LATEX along with their visual representation.


Findings

  • Our suggested approach consistently demonstrates the lowest average GPE rate among all compared techniques across especially low SNRs in presence of Babble noise & Train noise.
  • No time consuming and complicated post-processing of the signal waveform is needed.
  • Achieve more smooth signal and overcome the vocal tract effect efficiently by equalizing unnecessary ripples in the waveform.
